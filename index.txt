<h1>TimeTree Sync</h1>
<p>TimeTree Sync is a small personal tool that copies one family's TimeTree calendar into a separate Google Calendar, "Family (TimeTree)", once a night. It runs only on the owner's own Windows computer and is not offered to the public.</p>
<p>It can only see and change calendars it created itself. It cannot read or change any other calendar in the Google account.</p>
